Who I am

I started with a strong foundation in mathematics and statistics, completing an MSc in Mathematics at Mithibai College. I then moved into applied data science through a certified course at StarAgile and IBM certifications in Python and machine learning.

Since then I have worked across the full path from raw data to decisions. I build classification and forecasting models, automate ETL and reporting workflows, and design interactive dashboards in Tableau and Power BI. I also write backend services in Python, so models can move out of notebooks and into systems people use.

I joined SAIG as an intern in February 2026 and moved into an Analyst role in August 2026. My work there focuses on backend development, data pipelines, and reporting.
